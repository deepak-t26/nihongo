1. Interview Strategy

This job is not simply asking “Can you code in C#?” The description combines software development, product implementation, application support, customer communication, requirements gathering, troubleshooting, and Japanese communication.

① Show your transferable experience

You already work on healthcare/rehabilitation software. Emphasize that you understand software used in a medical environment, where accuracy, reliability, testing, and user feedback matter.

② Be honest about LIMS

If you have not worked directly on a LIMS, say so clearly. Then immediately explain your related experience and willingness to learn laboratory workflows.

③ Connect your .NET experience

Talk about your C#/.NET learning and .NET MAUI/internal-tool exposure. Do not describe yourself as an expert if your professional experience is mainly Flutter.

④ Demonstrate customer-facing behavior

Use phrases such as 「確認します」「認識を合わせます」「原因を切り分けます」. These are very useful for support/implementation roles.

Key sentence to remember:
「LIMSの実務経験はありませんが、医療・リハビリ分野のアプリ開発経験があり、要件確認から設計、開発、テスト、保守まで経験しています。これまでの経験を活かしながら、LIMSの業務知識を身につけたいと考えています。」
LIMS no jitsu-mu keiken wa arimasen ga, iryō / rihabiri bun'ya no apuri kaihatsu keiken ga ari, yōken kakunin kara sekkei, kaihatsu, tesuto, hoshu made keiken shiteimasu. Kore made no keiken o ikashinagara, LIMS no gyōmu chishiki o mi ni tsuketai to kangaeteimasu.
I don't have practical LIMS experience, but I have experience developing healthcare/rehabilitation applications, from requirements confirmation through design, development, testing, and maintenance. I would like to use my previous experience while learning LIMS business knowledge.

3. LIMS: What You Need to Understand for the Interview

A LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System) manages laboratory information and workflows. It can handle samples, tests, results, users, instruments, reports, and audit/history information.

サンプル管理 Sample management
Registering and tracking samples.
検査管理 Test management
Managing tests performed on samples.
結果管理 Result management
Recording, validating, and storing results.
ワークフロー Workflow
Sample received → test → review → approval.
機器連携 Instrument integration
Connecting laboratory instruments and software.
監査証跡 Audit trail
Tracking who changed what and when.

C. Requirements / customer communication

Clarifying requirements
認識の違いがないように、まず要件を確認したいです。
Ninshiki no chigai ga nai yō ni, mazu yōken o kakunin shitai desu.
I would first like to confirm the requirements so that there is no difference in understanding.
When something is unclear

Sumimasen, mō ichido go-setsumei itadakemasu deshō ka.

Confirming your understanding

Watashi no rikai de wa, 〇〇 to iu ninshiki desu ga, atteimasu deshō ka.
My understanding is that it is 〇〇. Is that correct?

D. Troubleshooting

Investigating an issue

Gen'in o chōsa shita kekka, dēta no tōroku shori ni mondai ga aru koto ga wakarimashita.

7. Technical Questions You May Get

What is LIMS?
LIMSは、Laboratory Information Management Systemの略で、検査室のサンプル、検査結果、ユーザー、機器、データなどを管理するシステムです。
LIMS stands for Laboratory Information Management System. It manages laboratory samples, test results, users, instruments, and data.
Why is database knowledge important for LIMS?
LIMSでは、サンプル情報や検査結果など、多くのデータを正確に管理する必要があります。そのため、データベースの設計、検索、更新、整合性などが重要だと考えています。
LIMS needs to accurately manage large amounts of data such as sample information and test results. Therefore database design, querying, updating, and data consistency are important.
What is the difference between a bug and an operational issue?
不具合はシステムの想定された動作と実際の動作が違う場合です。運用上の問題の場合は、設定やデータ、利用方法などが原因の場合もあります。まず状況を確認して原因を切り分けます。
A bug is when actual behavior differs from expected system behavior. An operational issue can also be caused by configuration, data, or usage. I would first investigate the situation and isolate the cause.
What is API integration?
API連携は、異なるシステム同士がAPIを使ってデータを送受信することです。例えば、外部システムから検査データを取得したり、結果を別のシステムに送ったりする場合があります。
API integration means different systems exchange data through APIs. For example, a system may retrieve test data from an external system or send results to another system.
What would you consider when developing a healthcare/laboratory system?
データの正確性、アクセス権限、セキュリティ、ログ、テスト、変更履歴などが重要だと考えています。また、ユーザーが間違えにくい画面や業務フローも重要です。
I think data accuracy, access permissions, security, logs, testing, and change history are important. Usable screens and workflows that reduce user errors are also important.

8. Technical Support / Implementation Questions

「お客様から『システムが遅い』と言われたら、どうしますか？」

If a customer says “the system is slow,” what would you do?
まず、いつから発生しているか、どの画面や処理で発生するかを確認します。その後、ログ、DB、API、ネットワークなどを確認して、原因を切り分けます。
First, I would confirm when it started and which screen or process is affected. Then I would check logs, the database, APIs, network, etc. to narrow down the cause.

「お客様の要望と仕様が合わない場合は？」

What if the customer's request does not match the current specification?
まず現在の仕様とお客様の目的を確認します。その上で、変更が必要か、別の方法で対応できるかを検討します。影響範囲やスケジュールも確認して、チームと相談します。
First I would confirm the current specification and the customer's objective. Then I would consider whether a change is necessary or whether another solution is possible. I would also check the impact and schedule and discuss it with the team.

「日本語で技術的な説明をするのが難しい場合は？」

専門用語をできるだけ簡単な言葉にして説明します。また、画面や資料を使って説明し、最後に相手の理解を確認します。分からない場合は、無理に答えず確認してから回答します。
I explain technical terms using simpler language as much as possible. I also use screens or documents and confirm the other person's understanding. If I do not know something, I confirm it before answering rather than guessing.

9. Japanese Communication Survival Kit

For this interview, you do not need complicated Japanese. You need to show that you can listen, clarify, confirm, and communicate responsibly.

Do not say: 「分かりません。」 and stop.
